# Refund & Billing Policy

- Clients on monthly retainers may request a pro-rated refund within 7 days of a billing date if no work has been delivered against that cycle.
- Refunds for completed milestones are not issued; disputed milestone quality is handled via revision rounds first (up to 2 free revisions).
- Any refund over $500 requires founder/account-lead sign-off before being communicated to the client.
- Chargeback threats or legal language from a client must always be escalated to a human, never auto-resolved.
