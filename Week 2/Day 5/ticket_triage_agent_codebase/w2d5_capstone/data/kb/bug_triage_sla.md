# Bug Triage SLA

- Critical (production down, data loss, security issue): acknowledge within 1 hour, fix or mitigate within 24 hours.
- High (major feature broken, no workaround): acknowledge within 4 hours, fix within 3 business days.
- Medium (feature partially broken, workaround exists): acknowledge within 1 business day, fix within 1-2 sprints.
- Low (cosmetic, minor): acknowledge within 2 business days, scheduled into backlog.
- Security-related bugs are always Critical and must be escalated to a human immediately, regardless of client tone.
