# Client Onboarding Checklist

1. Send welcome email with kickoff call link.
2. Collect access credentials (repo, hosting, domain) via the secure intake form -- never ask for passwords in plain chat/email.
3. Confirm scope, timeline, and milestone payment schedule.
4. Assign a project lead and share the shared Slack/Discord channel.
5. Schedule a 30-minute kickoff call within 3 business days of contract signing.
